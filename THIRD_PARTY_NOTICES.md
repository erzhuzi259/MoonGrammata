# Third-party notices and idea provenance

MoonGrammata contains no copied or translated third-party implementation code.
Its MoonBit source, tests, grammars, fixtures, and examples were written for this
project.

The following primary sources informed terminology, product boundaries, and
algorithm selection:

- LLVM libFuzzer documentation, Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions:
  <https://llvm.org/docs/LibFuzzer.html>
- Google structure-aware fuzzing guide, Apache-2.0 repository:
  <https://github.com/google/fuzzing/blob/master/docs/structure-aware-fuzzing.md>
- Google OSS-Fuzz documentation and examples, Apache-2.0 repository:
  <https://github.com/google/oss-fuzz>
- The Fuzzing Book grammar and reduction chapters, MIT license:
  <https://www.fuzzingbook.org/>

These works are cited as background. MoonGrammata does not redistribute their
source, test corpora, generated code, or binaries.
